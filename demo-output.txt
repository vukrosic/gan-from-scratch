  "pull" = the length of the arrow that the forger's goal draws on one fake (the slope of the goal at the fake).

Three saved runs, spots at the end:
  32 random numbers: 1 1 1
   2 random numbers: 8 8 8
Why could that be? A point on the page has 2 numbers. With 2 numbers in, the forger only has to bend the page.
With 32 numbers in, it has to press 32 directions flat first, and one clump is the easiest way to do that.
This is a guess, not a proof. Next exercise: try 4, 8 and 16 (the word noise in train) and find where the clump comes back.
